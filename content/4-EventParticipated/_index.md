During my internship, I participated in 3 events, each of which was a memorable experience providing new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with wonderful gifts and moments. Below is a detailed description of the events I attended:

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Knowledge Sharing Session

&emsp;**Time:** June 13, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** In-person

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee 

&emsp;**Event Description:** <br>
&emsp;This was the first event I attended in person, featuring 4 highly practical sharing topics revolving around technical skills and career orientation:<br>
&emsp;1. **"From First Cloud AI Journey to AWS Partner"** presented by Mr. Danh Hoang Hieu Nghi (AI Engineer, AWS Community Builder).<br>
&emsp;2. **"What does a DevOps Engineer really do?"** – Delving into the actual daily work of a DevOps engineer.<br>
&emsp;3. **"A scalable URL shortening service on AWS"** – A classic System Design problem presented by Dinh Trung Kien and Nguyen Minh Tho.<br>
&emsp;4. **"Real stories to culture at a multinational corporation"** – Valuable insights from the perspectives of Mr. Dat Pham (Data Analytics Engineer) and Mr. Cuong Nguyen (Process Engineer).

&emsp;**Outcomes / Value Gained:**  
&emsp;Through this event, I gained an overview of the career development path on the cloud platform. The knowledge about AWS system architecture design (URL shortening) and the realistic picture of the DevOps profession greatly enriched my technical mindset. Furthermore, the stories about professional working culture provided immense motivation for me to better prepare for the upcoming journey.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS First Cloud AI Journey Community Day - June 2026

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 – 12:00 | Saturday, June 27, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Online via YouTube (Original event held at Bitexco Financial Tower, HCMC)

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee 

&emsp;**Event Description:**  
&emsp;The event brought new perspectives on the AI wave shaping the future of enterprises. The content included in-depth sharing sessions and practical demos on: the "Cloud Thinker" mindset, building Voice AI for Vietnamese language processing, applying Dev Agent to automate DevOps and reduce Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR), applying Amazon Q in Human Resources (HR) operations, and secure Amazon Q deployment techniques using an MCP server.

&emsp;**Outcomes / Value Gained:**  
&emsp;I understood how enterprises actually deploy GenAI in practical operations rather than just at the conceptual level. The biggest lesson was grasping the importance of incorporating a "Human-in-the-loop" mechanism in AI workflows and private data security methods for enterprises when integrating with Large Language Models (LLMs).

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Onboarding & Knowledge Sharing Session: Agentic AI

&emsp;**Time:** August 1, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** In-person

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee 

&emsp;**Event Description:**  
&emsp;The event was a practical onboarding session combined with in-depth training on Agentic AI led by Mr. Nghia and Hai Anh. The sharing session delved into distinguishing the levels of AI autonomy, breaking down the 3 core architectural layers of Amazon Bedrock Agent Core (Runtime Environment, Identity & Access Management, and Gateway). Additionally, the event included a Hands-on Lab for attendees to directly configure and practice safe operational strategies (Best Practices) for enterprises.

&emsp;**Outcomes / Value Gained:**  
&emsp;Through the event, not only did I experience the professional workspace and culture directly at the AWS headquarters, but I also mastered the mindset for designing large-scale AI systems. The most valuable lesson was clearly understanding the importance of security from the network layer (PrivateLink) to virtualization (Firecracker), the "Human-in-the-loop" moderation mechanism, and how architectural layers coordinate to create a secure and flexible Agentic AI system.
